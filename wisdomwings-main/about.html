Meet the Owner — Renuka Vasu, Founder

Wisdom Wings Healthcare is a Medical Coding Training and Placement company dedicated to helping students build successful careers in the healthcare industry. Our goal is to provide quality training, practical knowledge, and the right guidance for individuals who want to grow in the field of medical coding.

The company was founded by Renuka Vasu (R V), who brings over 23 years of professional experience, including 3 years in the healthcare sector. Her journey in medical coding began under strong mentorship and a commitment to continuous learning. She remains deeply grateful to her mentor, whose guidance and support gave her the freedom to understand the role in depth and build the confidence needed to progress successfully in this field.

For the past few years, the work has been carried out through various medical coding services and support activities. With the growing experience and vision to help more aspiring coders, these services have now been officially established under the name Wisdom Wings Healthcare.

At Wisdom Wings Healthcare, we believe that knowledge gives you wings to grow in your career. Our mission is to train, guide, and support students so they can confidently step into the medical coding industry.

Learn Medical Coding and fly high with the support of Wisdom Wings Healthcare. We are here to help you build your future and reach new heights in your career.
